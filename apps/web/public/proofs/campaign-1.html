How the sponsored escrow works

  1. The sponsor creates the campaign and deposits 1 simulated Studionet GEN into the Intelligent Contract.
  2. The named creator accepts the frozen terms, then publishes and submits a public evidence URL from the campaign's allowed origin.
  3. GenLayer validators reach consensus by checking creator identity, publication timing, required content, and sponsorship disclosure against the on-chain brief and rubric.
  4. A PASS permits the contract to pay the creator; a FAIL or missed deadline makes the sponsor eligible for a refund. UNRESOLVED remains on-chain and supports bounded retry or eventual recovery.
